Mossglen

A sunleaf grows beyond the eastern bridge. Speak to the village healer, face the moss slime, and bring the leaf home.

Play Luau →Luau game · C++ player Play Odin →Odin game · Odin player

Both versions use the same art, map, and rules. Arrows or WASD to walk; Space to talk and confirm. In battle, use arrows to select Attack, Guard, or Potion.

384 × 216 · One quest · About five minutes · Progress resets when you reload.
